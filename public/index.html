03 / Approach

We treat engineering as a design discipline, not a checking service. We are in the room from the first sketch to the last bolt.

Merkel was founded on a simple idea: the best-performing structures are engineered early, while the design is still cheap to change. We work alongside architects and clients from day one, running analysis as the design develops rather than after it.

  1. /01

    Interrogate the brief

    Loads, ground, climate and constraints are mapped before a single line is drawn.

  2. /02

    Model as one source of truth

    Every discipline works from a shared parametric model and digital twin.

  3. /03

    Optimise, then prove

    We reduce material and carbon, then verify with full non-linear analysis.

  4. /04

    Engineer to site

    Detailing and site support keep the design intact through construction.
