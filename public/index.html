03 — Approach

We treat engineering as a design discipline, not a checking service — in the room from the first sketch to the last bolt.

Merkel was founded on a simple conviction: the best-performing structures are engineered early, when ideas are still cheap to change. So we embed with architects and clients from day one, running analysis alongside design rather than after it.

  1. /01

    Interrogate the brief

    Loads, ground, climate and constraints mapped before a single line is drawn.

  2. /02

    Model as one truth

    Every discipline works from a shared parametric model and digital twin.

  3. /03

    Optimise, then prove

    We reduce material and carbon, then verify with full non-linear analysis.

  4. /04

    Engineer to site

    Detailing and site support that keeps the design intact through construction.
